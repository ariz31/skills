# Independent Goal Auditor contract

Use after both Delegate cycles, and after every outer remediation round. Inspect the exact final candidate rather than a producer summary. A fresh independent reviewer must not have participated in creation, assembly or previous remediation; avoid priming with prior grades. Give it original requirements and constraints, candidate identifier and actual verification access. Prior evidence can be inspected after initial assessment but independently corroborate material claims. Separate role passes without real isolation are SELF-REVIEW.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Requirement coverage and holistic goal attainment | 3 |
| Technical accuracy, grounding and evidence | 3 |
| Requested format and actual usability | 2 |
| Polish, clarity and integration | 2 |
| Total | 10 |

Score based on evidence; no rounding up to ten. Check each applicable selected skill gate separately; never average away failures. A perfect scoped score does not establish universal absence of defects. PASS requires exactly 10/10, GOAL_ACHIEVED true, no outstanding defects, no unverified essential requirement, all applicable gates passed on this candidate, and actual independence when required. A failed prerequisite, missing essential proof or hard blocker overrides the score.

## Report

- AUDIT MODE: INDEPENDENT or SELF-REVIEW
- ARTIFACT: exact path/revision/content identifier
- GOAL_ACHIEVED: true or false
- SCORE: X/10
- VERDICT: PASS or FAIL
- PROOF: actual checks and observations, expected vs observed where applicable
- DEFECTS: IDs, severity, requirement, evidence, correction, owner
- TRACEABILITY: requirement → evidence → PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED
- UNVERIFIED: checks not performed and why; mark essentiality
- OTHER GATES: skill, score, verdict, candidate identifier

No fabricated proof, unsupported certification, or claim of independence for sequential self-review. Respect authorization and test exclusions.

## Gap Report

When blocked or the shared budget/three-round limit is exhausted, report:
- Original goal and exact candidate/partial output.
- Cycle 1/2 and goal audit results, mode, other gates and completed rounds.
- Outstanding requirement IDs, defects, evidence, unverified checks, and blocker.
- What was verified and usable, what remains provisional, and the smallest concrete next action.
- No claim that the goal was achieved or that the candidate is certified.
