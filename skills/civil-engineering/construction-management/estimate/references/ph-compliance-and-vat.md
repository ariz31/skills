# Philippine compliance, permits and VAT

Planning checklist, not a current fee schedule or legal/tax determination. Verify applicable editions, IRRs, local ordinances and tax rules for location/date from LGU/OBO, BFP, BIR and relevant agencies. Cite actual official source, date, jurisdiction and applicability; never invent clauses, fees or approvals.

## Permit schedule

PD 1096 (National Building Code) and applicable IRR, RA 9514 (Fire Code) and applicable IRR, and local ordinances are starting points. Check actual applicability.

| Permit / approval | Authority | Timing / basis |
|---|---|---|
| Barangay and zoning/locational clearances | Barangay/LGU | Applicable pre-building approvals; local schedule |
| Building permit and ancillary architectural, civil/structural, electrical, mechanical, sanitary/plumbing and other applicable permits | OBO | Scope, area/use and applicable fee schedule |
| Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) | BFP | Applicable plan evaluation before building permit |
| Excavation, demolition, fencing, road occupancy, signage | OBO/LGU/road authority | Only if triggered by scope |
| Environmental, heritage, utility, drainage or other site-specific approvals | Relevant agencies | Only when applicable; distinguish deposits |
| Certificate of occupancy and inspections | OBO/LGU | Completion/occupancy |
| Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) | BFP | Applicable inspection/occupancy or operation; not FSEC |

Track ID, authority, governing rule/edition, status (pending, secured-paid, secured-unpaid, not applicable, unknown), fee basis, paid amount, incremental payable, source/date, payee and tax category. Separate professional/processing charges from statutory fees; avoid overhead duplication. Secured-and-paid permit: zero incremental cost only with evidence or explicit user confirmation; retain historical cost if known. Secured-unpaid remains payable. Waived/not applicable needs basis. Unknown fees are not zero. Track refundable deposits separately; check renewals/change orders.

## VAT and pass-through

Use the 12% standard Philippine VAT planning rate only for verified applicable taxable supplies. Confirm contractor/supplier VAT registration, exemptions, zero-rating, recoverability, quote tax inclusion and reimbursement/agency arrangement. Government fees and reimbursements are not automatically exempt: distinguish documented agency disbursements from taxable contractor charges and markups. Non-VAT does not necessarily mean no other taxes.

- Tax-exclusive taxable quote: net = quote; VAT = net × rate; gross = net + VAT.
- Tax-inclusive taxable quote: net = gross / (1 + rate); VAT = gross − net. Do not multiply gross by 12% again.
- Exempt, zero-rated or outside-scope pass-through: separate categories and verified basis; no standard VAT added.
- Non-VAT supplier: retain actual cost; do not invent input VAT. Contractor output VAT may still apply to taxable contract billing.
- Choose owner procurement cash cost or contractor billing basis; distinguish recoverable input VAT, nonrecoverable cost and output VAT. Document markup/contingency tax base and avoid double taxation.
- Net/gross toggle affects presentation, not legal liability. Show excluded payable tax separately; for unresolved classifications provide conditional alternatives and seek qualified advice.

Illustration: taxable net PHP 10,000 plus verified outside-scope PHP 1,000 disbursement gives PHP 1,200 VAT and PHP 12,200 payable. A PHP 11,200 VAT-inclusive supply already includes PHP 1,200 VAT. If the disbursement is taxable, reclassify and recalculate.

Official starting points: [BIR](https://www.bir.gov.ph/), [BFP](https://bfp.gov.ph/), [DPWH](https://www.dpwh.gov.ph/) and project LGU/OBO. Retrieve the actual applicable document or disclose unavailable verification.
